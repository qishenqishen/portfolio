Qi Shen / portfolio MEALOG PRODUCT DEMO

slow social / meal memory / product prototype

Meals become memories when people, places, and small feelings stay connected.

Mealog is a soft social product concept: log a meal, remember who was there, and let the month slowly turn into a table of relationships.

0meal memories 0people around the table 0places remembered
01 Low-pressure sharing

No public feed by default. Memories can stay private, shared with one person, or become a monthly table.

02 Relationship-aware logging

The product cares less about calories and more about company, context, rhythm, and emotional texture.

03 AI-assisted recap

Instead of writing for the user, Mealog gently summarizes patterns the user may not have noticed.
